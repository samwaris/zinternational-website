About us

A registered company that works as an extension of your office

Z International is the trading name of Zaytun International Private Limited, a management service provider based in Kolkata. We take care of the administration behind a business, so our clients can focus on their customers and their core work.

The business began in 2019 as Brighton Inc., founded in Kolkata by Syed S M Waris. In 2022 it became Zaytun International Private Limited, carrying forward the same team, clients and experience. Since 2019 we have supported Nationwide Appliance Repairs in Australia with invoicing, customer enquiries, bookings and day-to-day support.

Our team follows each client's processes, works in the client's own systems and represents the client's business to its customers with care.

Privacy policy

How we handle your personal data

Last updated: 16 November 2026. This policy explains how Zaytun International Private Limited, trading as Z International ("we", "us"), handles personal data collected through this website, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect the details you enter: your name, business name, role, email, phone number, country, industry, team size, the services you are interested in, your message and your contact preferences.

When you apply for a job we collect your name, email, mobile number, city, the position you are applying for, your experience, your message and your CV.

Why we collect it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry, arrange a consultation and prepare a proposal for you. Job application details are used only to assess your application and contact you about it. We do not sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing.

Where it is stored

Enquiries are stored in our company Google Workspace account, which is accessible only to authorised staff of Z International.

How long we keep it

If we do not go on to work together, we delete enquiry details within 12 months. If you become a client, your details become part of our client records. Job applications and CVs are kept for up to 12 months so we can consider you for future openings, unless you ask us to delete them sooner; if you join us, they become part of your employee records.

Your rights

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct, update or delete your data.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if it is not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

Contact

For any privacy request or grievance, email admin@zinternational.co or write to our registered office at Room No. 15 (MB)/1, First Floor, 22B Rabindra Sarani, Kolkata, West Bengal 700073, India.
